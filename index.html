What is an AI chatbot?

A program that can answer visitor questions, guide conversations and capture leads while your business is offline.

Can it be trained on my business information?

Yes. FAQs, documents and website content can be used to shape the assistant's knowledge.

What platforms can it work on?

Website deployment is primary. Messenger, WhatsApp and Instagram can be added depending on the project.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Lead Flow Automation handles implementation.

What happens when the bot cannot answer?

It should not invent an answer. It can offer human assistance and route the request to David.

The project is a one-time cost. Optional maintenance is $200/month.
