Questions

Good to know.

A few answers before we start.

What kind of businesses can you help?

The systems can be adapted for service businesses, agencies, professional services, local businesses and other teams that receive repeated customer enquiries.

Will the chatbot sound like my business?

Yes. The conversation can be structured around your tone, services, FAQs, policies and the actions you want visitors to take.

Can you connect the chatbot to my existing tools?

Depending on the tools involved, lead information can be routed to email, spreadsheets, CRM systems and other supported workflows.

Is the demo on this page a real AI assistant?

This public demo is intentionally lightweight and uses predefined browser-side responses. Client systems can use a real AI backend and business-specific knowledge.
