Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

Information we may collect

If you contact Lead Flow Automation, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, business details and project requirements.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, qualify potential projects, prepare proposals, provide services and communicate about requested work.

Chat information

For future production chatbot deployments, chat logs should be retained only as long as needed for the agreed service. The current target for this project is 90 days, followed by deletion where technically supported.

Consent

Where contact information is requested through the chatbot, the planned flow asks for consent before storing information for follow-up. You can decline and continue browsing without providing contact details.

Third parties

Specific client deployments may use hosting, analytics, CRM, email or automation providers. Those providers will depend on the final project configuration.
